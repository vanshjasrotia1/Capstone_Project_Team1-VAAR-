# Capstone_Project_Team1-VAAR-
Smart Patient Readmission & Care Intervention Platform
Overview
This project was developed to help healthcare organizations reduce avoidable 30-day readmissions, improve patient outcomes, and optimize care management decisions using Analytics, Machine Learning, NLP, Generative AI, and Power BI.

The solution combines predictive analytics, patient segmentation, discharge-note analysis, and a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant to support clinical and operational decision-making.

Business Objectives
Identify patients at high risk of readmission
Understand factors driving readmissions
Segment patients based on risk profiles
Analyze discharge notes for actionable insights
Support care managers with an AI assistant
Monitor performance through executive dashboards
